"A what??."

a 'zine, or "fanzine," if you're not into the whole brevity thing, is what people used to call blogs. They were printed, tactile, independent, usually photocopied and sometimes handwritten. They could be about anything. They were topical, they were random, they were about Evan Dando of the Lemonheads, they were about dishwashing. And thats what you have here; The bars, hotels, restaurants, wines, designs and opinions we feel passionately about.
